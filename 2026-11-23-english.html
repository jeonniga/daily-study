Community Gardens and Cooperation

독해 0/3 · 어법 0/5 · 전체 0/8 · 채점 0/3

독해 지문

Community gardens can improve urban neighborhoods. Residents share small plots where they grow vegetables and flowers. These spaces also give neighbors opportunities to meet and exchange practical knowledge.

However, a successful garden requires more than enthusiasm. Volunteers must agree on schedules and maintain shared equipment. Without clear responsibilities, even a popular project may gradually lose support.

Some schools cooperate with local gardens to teach environmental science. Students observe how soil, water, and sunlight affect plant growth. Although gardens cannot solve every urban problem, they can strengthen community ties and encourage careful use of resources.
