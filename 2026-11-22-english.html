독해 지문

Urban trees do more than make streets attractive. During hot weather, their shade can lower the temperature experienced by pedestrians. However, planting trees without a long-term plan may create new problems.

A city must consider the space available for roots and the amount of water each species requires. If trees are planted too close to buildings, their roots may damage underground structures. Regular inspections also help workers identify branches that could become dangerous during storms.

Successful programs involve residents as well as experts. When neighbors help care for young trees, they often develop a stronger sense of responsibility for shared spaces. Such cooperation does not replace professional maintenance, but it can make public projects more sustainable.
