독해 지문

Community gardens offer city residents a place to grow vegetables and meet their neighbors. People who have little access to green space can benefit from these shared areas. However, a garden needs more than land and seeds to remain successful.

Each group of gardeners agrees on rules before the growing season begins. Experienced members teach newcomers how to prepare the soil and save water. By sharing responsibilities, participants can prevent a few people from doing all the work.

Although disagreements sometimes arise, regular meetings help members find solutions. The vegetables that are harvested may be shared with local food banks. In this way, community gardens can strengthen social connections as well as improve access to fresh food.
