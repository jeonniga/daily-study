정상 영어 지문

When a town opened a small repair cafe, its organizers expected residents to bring broken household items. Instead, many visitors arrived simply to learn how ordinary devices worked. The volunteers explained that understanding a problem was often more valuable than fixing it immediately.

At first, some participants were reluctant to ask questions because they feared appearing inexperienced. However, the workshops encouraged everyone to describe what they had already tried. As people exchanged ideas, they became more confident in making careful decisions. The organizers also kept records of common problems so that future sessions could address them.

The project did not promise that every object could be repaired. Its larger purpose was to make practical knowledge accessible and to reduce unnecessary waste. By the end of the year, several visitors had begun teaching newcomers, showing how shared learning can strengthen a community.
